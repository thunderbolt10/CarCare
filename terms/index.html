CarCare – Terms and Conditions

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. About CarCare

CarCare is a personal, non-commercial program that reads status information from the owner's own Volvo car through the Volvo Cars Connected Vehicle API and displays it on the owner's computer. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by Volvo Cars.

2. Who may use it

CarCare is intended only for use by its author with vehicles linked to the author's own Volvo ID. No accounts are offered to the public.

3. Read-only access

CarCare only requests read access to vehicle data (for example odometer, fuel, tyre, door, window, warning and diagnostic status). It does not send commands to the vehicle.

4. Data and privacy

  • Vehicle data is fetched on demand and shown on the user's own computer only.
  • No vehicle or personal data is sold, shared with third parties or sent to any server other than Volvo Cars' APIs.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the user's computer and can be deleted at any time.
  • Access can be revoked at any time through the user's Volvo ID account.

5. No warranty

CarCare is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. Vehicle data may be delayed, incomplete or inaccurate and must not be relied on for safety-critical decisions. Always follow the vehicle's own displays and warnings.

6. Limitation of liability

7. Use of the Volvo Cars API

Use of CarCare is also subject to the Volvo Cars developer terms and the Volvo ID terms of use.
